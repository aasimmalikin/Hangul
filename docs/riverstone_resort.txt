Riverstone Mountain Resort — Guest Information Guide

OVERVIEW
Riverstone Mountain Resort is located in the Cascade Mountains at an elevation of 6,200 feet.
The resort opened in 1998 and has 180 guest rooms across three lodges.
The resort sits on 450 acres of private land.
The general manager is Katherine Ellis.

ACCOMMODATION
The three lodges are named Pinecrest, Summit, and Lakeside.
Pinecrest Lodge has 80 rooms and is the largest.
Summit Lodge has 60 rooms and offers the best mountain views.
Lakeside Lodge has 40 rooms and sits next to Emerald Lake.
Standard room rate is 240 dollars per night.
A suite costs 480 dollars per night.
Check-in time is 4:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM.

DINING
The resort has 4 restaurants.
The flagship restaurant is called The Copper Kettle.
The Copper Kettle serves dinner only and requires reservations.
Breakfast is included with every room booking.
The resort cafe is open from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

ACTIVITIES
In winter, the resort operates 12 ski runs and 3 chairlifts.
The longest ski run is 2.5 miles.
In summer, guests can access 25 miles of hiking trails.
The resort offers guided kayaking on Emerald Lake.
A spa with 8 treatment rooms is open year-round.
The resort has an indoor heated pool and two hot tubs.

POLICIES
Pets are allowed in Lakeside Lodge only, for an extra 50 dollars per night.
The resort has a strict no-smoking policy in all buildings.
Cancellations must be made 48 hours in advance for a full refund.
The resort provides free shuttle service to the nearest town, 15 miles away.
Wi-Fi is complimentary throughout the property.
