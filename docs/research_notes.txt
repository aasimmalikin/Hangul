Project Aurora — Research Notes

Project Aurora is studying battery energy density improvements.
The current prototype achieves 320 watt-hours per kilogram.
The target for 2027 is 500 watt-hours per kilogram.
The research team consists of 12 scientists.
Testing is conducted across 3 laboratory sites.
The lead researcher is Dr. Sofia Andersson.
The project has a budget of 8 million dollars over 3 years.
Each test cycle takes 6 weeks to complete.
So far, 14 test cycles have been completed.
The prototype uses a silicon-anode design.
