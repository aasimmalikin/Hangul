Harbor Freight Logistics — Q4 Operations Report

FLEET
The company operates a fleet of 54 delivery trucks.
Each truck completes an average of 12 deliveries per day.
The fleet covers 3 regional zones.

WAREHOUSE
The central warehouse spans 85,000 square feet.
It holds 14 loading docks.
Peak daily throughput is 9,200 packages.

STAFFING
There are 145 warehouse staff and 54 drivers.
The operations director is Priya Nair.

PERFORMANCE
On-time delivery rate was 94 percent in Q4.
Average cost per delivery was 6 dollars and 50 cents.
