Nimbus Analytics — FY2025 Financial Summary

Nimbus Analytics reported total revenue of 48 million dollars in fiscal year 2025.
Cost of goods sold was 18 million dollars.
Operating expenses totaled 22 million dollars.
The company has 320 full-time employees across 4 offices.
The headquarters is located in Austin, Texas.
Nimbus closed 3 acquisition deals during the year.
The average deal size was 2.4 million dollars.
Cash reserves at year end stood at 15 million dollars.
The Chief Financial Officer is Elena Vasquez.
The company plans to hire 80 additional engineers in 2026.
