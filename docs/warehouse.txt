Meridian Logistics Warehouse Reference

The Riverside warehouse has 12 storage aisles. Each aisle holds 45 pallet racks, and each pallet rack can store 8 pallets.

A standard shipping pallet at Meridian weighs 27 kilograms when empty. When fully loaded, a pallet carries 340 kilograms of goods.

The warehouse operates 3 shifts per day. Each shift has 14 workers, and each worker processes an average of 62 packages per shift.

Delivery trucks at the Riverside facility have a maximum cargo capacity of 9500 kilograms per trip.
